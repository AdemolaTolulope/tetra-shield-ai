    About, Methods & Scientific Honesty

    Platform & name

    BioShield-X = the Environmental BioAI decision platform (contaminant X is a variable). TETRA-SHIELD is the first fully implemented module (tetracycline). Naming analysis in docs/NAMING.md — the name was retained because the expansion is meaningful (Tetracycline Environmental Transformation, Resistance & Safety Hazard Intelligence Engine), not because it sounds impressive.

    Where computation actually runs

    • Molecular docking: smina (AutoDock Vina 1.1.2 scoring), executed in this repo at build time; protocol validated by re-docking the co-crystallised ligand (RMSD 1.84 Å).
    • Cheminformatics: RDKit descriptors, Morgan-fingerprint Tanimoto, structural-alert SMARTS.
    • Sequence analysis: Biopython global alignments (identity %).
    • Decision engine: deterministic evidence-integration scoring (ML deliberately not fitted on n=6 evidence-graded candidates — see MODEL_CARD).
    • Sensitivity: Monte-Carlo weight perturbation (400 sets) + Kendall's W rank concordance.

    Limitations (read before citing)

    • 6 candidates, 1 contaminant — a first module, not a coverage claim.
    • Component scores are documented heuristics tuned to the literature, not fitted parameters.
    • Product toxicity evidence is sparse by nature of the field — rendered as UNKNOWN, not "safe".
    • Nigerian occurrence data = point studies; no national generalisation.
