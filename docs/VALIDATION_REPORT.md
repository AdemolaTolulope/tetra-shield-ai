# VALIDATION REPORT — TETRA-SHIELD AI

Date: 2026-09-17 · Build: prototype v1.0 · Artifacts regenerated by scripts in `src/`.

## 1. Docking protocol validation (executed)
- Method: re-dock 7-chlortetracycline into its own binding site (PDB 2Y6R chain A + FAD,
  co-crystal ligand removed), smina / Vina 1.1.2, seed 42, exhaustiveness 16, 20 Å box
  on co-crystal centroid.
- RMSD method: element-aware Hungarian assignment + Kabsch on heavy atoms (19 pairs);
  necessary because the PDB ligand lacks CONECT records (RDKit substructure matching
  fails — documented in code).
- **Result: best-mode RMSD 1.84 Å → PASS (≤3.0 Å tolerance).**
- Consequence: tetracycline affinity (−9.7 kcal/mol) is computable with a validated
  protocol and is used as ONE component (substrate compatibility) of 13.

## 2. Decision-engine robustness (executed)
- Monte-Carlo sensitivity: 400 random weight sets, ±25% around BALANCED, seed 7.
- **Kendall's W = 0.955** (near-perfect rank concordance).
- Rank stability: TETX2-BT mean 1.00 (always top-2); LAC-TV mean 2.74 (always top-3);
  TETX-BF 2.37 (P(top-3) 0.89); BSFL-AA12 always rank 6 (honest floor under uncertainty).
- Ablation (verbatim from `results/decision_engine_validation.json`):
  - without AMR: TetX-family scores inflate to 97.9–100 → AMR layer has real effect;
  - without product safety: fungal enzymes overtake TetX (96.4 vs 78.3) → safety layer inverts a tradeoff;
  - without structure/uncertainty/circularity/environment: small shifts (2–5 pts) — reported, not hidden.
- Baselines: literature-only, sequence-family-only and integrated ordering all agree TETX2
  leads, but **only the integrated engine places LAC-TV above TETX3-PA** — the AMR/safety
  differentiation is precisely the value-add.

## 4. Leakage & overfitting statement
- No fitted ML ⇒ no train/test leakage. Deterministic similarity measures are reported
  as similarity, never as predictive performance.
- Where "validation-like" behaviour exists (re-docking), the reference is an
  *independent experimental truth* (co-crystal pose), not a self-consistency check.
